Click a table to see its owner, its columns as sealed in canon, and the laws that guard it.

Laws every table obeys

  • merchant_id first in every unique index on crm_* tables; platform_* tables never have one
  • SQL only in the owner's db/ other modules go through contracts; a boundary checker enforces it in CI
  • Invariants in tables append-only logs, immutable rows, set-once columns by trigger, not by discipline
  • No vocabulary CHECKs channels, connectors, topics live in code; only closed status ladders are CHECKed
  • No stored derived state a predicate can answer: expiry, in-window, monthly spend
